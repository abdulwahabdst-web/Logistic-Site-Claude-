F.A.Q

Straight answers, so you can move ahead with confidence.

01What does Novafreight do?

We are a freight forwarder and logistics provider. We plan and manage air freight, ocean freight, road transport, customs brokerage and warehousing as one connected service, with a single team accountable for every step.

02Which industries do you work with?

Retail and e-commerce, automotive and industrial equipment, mining and resources, building and construction, food and beverage, healthcare and consumer electronics, plus project and heavy-lift cargo.

03Which shipping modes do you offer?

Air (express, consolidated and charter), ocean (FCL, LCL and breakbulk) and road (metro, regional and interstate). We combine modes when that gives you a better balance of cost and speed.

04Can you handle customs clearance?

Yes. Our licensed in-house brokers manage tariff classification, duties and taxes, permits, quarantine and inspections, so your goods clear without surprises.

05How is freight pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on mode, route, volume or weight, cargo type and timing. Every quote is itemised and all-in, so you know exactly what you are paying for before you book.

06Do you provide warehousing and 3PL?

We offer short and long-term storage, pick and pack, kitting, cross-docking and distribution, connected directly to your inbound and outbound freight.

07Can you move oversized or heavy cargo?

Yes. Our project cargo team engineers out-of-gauge and heavy-lift moves end to end, including route surveys, permits, escorts and specialised equipment.

08How do I get a quote?

Send us your origin, destination, cargo details and timing through the contact form or by email. A specialist will come back to you within one business day.
